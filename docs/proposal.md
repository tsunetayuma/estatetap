# EstateTap

_One-tap investing in tokenized real estate, as easy as a mobile banking app_

## Summary

EstateTap lets anyone buy fractional shares of real estate properties on Solana with a single tap, using social/email login instead of seed phrases and instant fiat or stablecoin on-ramp. Rent income is auto-distributed to holders' wallets via Solana Pay, and a simple dashboard shows ownership and yield in real time.

## Target users

Retail investors and crypto beginners who want easy, low-friction exposure to real estate

## Problem

Real estate investing and crypto onboarding are both complex, scaring off everyday investors from RWA opportunities.

## Solution

Abstract away wallets and crypto complexity with embedded wallets, fiat on-ramp, and one-tap purchase of tokenized property shares.

## MVP features

- Email/social login creates embedded Solana wallet automatically
- One-tap purchase of fractional property tokens with fiat or USDC
- Automatic rent distribution to holders via Solana Pay
- Simple portfolio dashboard showing ownership % and yield history
- Demo property with mock SPV data pre-loaded for investor testing
